# Case notes (invented for the worked example)

Alex Example, 34, has had tiredness after light exercise for two years and pale skin patches on both arms.
Both parents carry one faulty copy of gene XA; Alex carries two.

Laboratory report: blood substrate S 48 µmol/L (normal 2–10).
Enzyme X assay in blood cells: 6 % of normal activity, below 10 % of normal.
Diet history: ordinary intake of substrate S, so a dietary excess does not explain the level.

Plan: weekly enzyme X replacement by infusion, and a low substrate S diet.
